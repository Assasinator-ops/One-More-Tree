## Design Philosophy Applied

### Flat Design Principles
- ✅ Solid colors over gradients (except hero backgrounds)
- ✅ Flat borders (2px solid) over blurred edges
- ✅ Subtle shadows (1-2px) over heavy shadows
- ✅ Geometric shapes over organic blobs
- ✅ Pattern overlays for texture (not blur effects)
- ✅ Clean typography (no blur or glow)

### Ergonomic Design
- ✅ Comfortable spacing (padding: 1.5rem, 2rem, 3rem)
- ✅ Readable typography (clamp(1rem, 1.25vw, 1.125rem))
- ✅ Touch targets (44px minimum for mobile)
- ✅ Consistent layout patterns
- ✅ Clear visual hierarchy
- ✅ Predictable interactions

### Modern & Elegant
- ✅ Fluid typography (responsive scaling)
- ✅ Consistent color palette (forest tones)
- ✅ Subtle animations (not jarring)
- ✅ Professional appearance
- ✅ Clean visual structure
