What we cannot do

We are happy to say no to the wrong request before it becomes a wrong promise. Some things we will not do, and some things we cannot do alone — and saying so plainly is how we keep the rest of this site honest.

  • We do not sell carbon offsets — every tree on this site is planted and kept for its own sake, and every survival figure is reported whether it looks good or not.
  • We do not plant without a plan — a plot is surveyed for at least six weeks before a single sapling is ordered, and the wrong tree in the wrong place is still the wrong tree.
  • We do not take anonymous donations — every contribution is recorded against a name and a plot, because anonymous money is not stewardship, and stewardship is the whole point.
  • We do not visit without making it real — a monitoring walk is a proper visit on a living plot, not a drive past a site that has not been checked in six months.

Clearer boundaries, clearer results.
