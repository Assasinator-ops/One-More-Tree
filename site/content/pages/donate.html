Other ways to give

Monthly rather than once

A recurring €15 covers roughly two hectares of stewardship a year and lets us plan planting seasons instead of reacting to them. Regular giving is worth considerably more to us than the same total in one payment.

Corporate sponsorship

Fund a full plot for five years and receive quarterly monitoring reports — including the losses. A named site, real data, and no pre-sold carbon credits. Your procurement team will have questions; we have answers.

Give your time instead

Not everyone can give money, and planting weeks genuinely need hands. Volunteers plant alongside local guardians and stay for the first summer's watering where they can.
