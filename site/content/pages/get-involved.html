There are three useful things you can do and one that is mostly useful to you. We would rather you picked from the first three.

Most effective

Fund a hectare

Money is the bottleneck and it always will be. €18 plants a hectare, €90 keeps one alive for a year, €450 takes a hectare all the way through its five-year establishment period.

You get quarterly monitoring reports on the plot you funded, including the trees that died.

Donate

Get your hands dirty

Volunteer

Planting weeks run October to March. No experience required — you work in a pair with a trained local guardian, and the first morning covers technique and safety.

Accommodation is included. Surveyors and GIS specialists are especially welcome.

Join a planting week

If you have land

Offer a plot

Municipalities, landowners and farmers with degraded or abandoned ground can nominate it for restoration. We survey first, and we will tell you honestly if the site is not viable.

We prioritise land that connects existing woodland, or that protects a watercourse or a slope above a village.

Nominate land

Foundations, municipalities and schools

We run co-funded programmes with public bodies and school groups, and we are happy to design a restoration programme around your region rather than sending you to ours. What we will not do is attach a logo to a hectare that we have not monitored.

Write to hello@onemoretree.eu or use the contact form with “Corporate partnership” selected.
